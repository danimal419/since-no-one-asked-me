I'll be completely honest with you; I'm not entirely sure what this website is going to look like.

I decided to start this website because I enjoy writing, and I wanted to learn how to do some basic coding. Put those two things together and bam; here we are. It's as simple as that.

One day, I might write about one of my favorite places to eat or a local open mic. The next day, I could rant about the latest nonsense coming out of the White House or my fleeting faith in humanity. If it's something that interests me or could potentially help someone out in some capacity, it's on the table.

I'm just a white dude in his forties from the midwest who's got some shit to say from time to time. Why not put it out there in hopes someone finds it interesting or, at the very least, entertaining?

Be sure to tune in for next week's episode of “Since No One Asked Me” to see what happens next.
